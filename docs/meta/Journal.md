# 📔 Journal.md

> Three minutes at the end of each session. This is the document that, six months from now, tells you how far you've actually come — which is the thing that is hardest to feel while it's happening.

---

## How to fill it in

Don't write a diary. Write **evidence**. The four columns that matter:

| Column | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| **Hours** | After Module 2 you can estimate your own speed. That is a professional skill and you cannot acquire it without data. |
| **What I reached for first** | Tells you what's become automatic. |
| **What I had to look up** | Tells you what hasn't. This column *is* your skill map. |
| **Note to future me** | The half-finished thought. Write it — future-you never remembers. |

> 💡 **The one that surprises people:** *what I had to look up* is more useful than *what I learned*. Anything you looked up twice belongs in a cheat sheet; anything you looked up three times belongs in [`Doubts.md`](Doubts.md) as a real gap.
